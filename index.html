About Philippa

A rare combination of technical depth, creative intelligence, and community leadership.

Philippa’s work sits at the intersection of geospatial intelligence, cybersecurity, urban planning, data, media, and strategic communication. She brings a broad view of systems together with the ability to explain difficult ideas clearly.

Her career includes technical analysis and development, creative direction, content and business strategy, public speaking, mentoring, and advocacy. She is equally comfortable working with maps and models, people and platforms, policy and storytelling.
